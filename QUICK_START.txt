TARTEEL STUDIO — QUICK START

1) Install Node.js 18 or newer from nodejs.org.
2) Windows: double-click start.bat
   macOS/Linux: open Terminal here and run ./start.sh
3) The first launch installs the required packages.
4) Open http://127.0.0.1:4173 in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
5) Select a surah, record or upload audio, press Next after each ayah,
   choose effects, then export MP3/WAV/MP4.

Read README.md for full instructions and troubleshooting.
