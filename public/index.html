IMPORTANT

Respectful use and accuracy

This tool enhances your own recording with cleanup, EQ, compression, ambience, and video graphics. It does not clone a real qāriʾ or claim your recitation is theirs.

It does not verify tajwīd, pronunciation, waqf, or correctness. Review exported text and audio carefully before publishing.

All recording and rendering happens on your computer through the local Node server. Nothing is sent to a third-party audio service.
